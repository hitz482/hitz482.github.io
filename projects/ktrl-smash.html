Overview

Ktrl Smash is a free-to-play online arena brawler. It launched on Steam in December 2025. As of November 2026, 73% of its 15 Steam reviews are positive (Mostly Positive). It also runs in the browser as a WebGL build.

My role

I built the multiplayer layer on Photon Fusion and worked with the devops team to deploy dedicated servers on AWS. After release I diagnosed and fixed a network performance problem that grew over the length of a session. It was a studio project, and the multiplayer layer and the ping fix are my contribution.

Main responsibilities

  • Multiplayer layer on Photon Fusion.
  • Dedicated server deployment on AWS, together with devops.
  • Network performance debugging for both the Windows and WebGL builds.

Technical challenges

Ping climbed during a session: from 20 to 60 ms on Windows and from 40 to 100 ms on WebGL. On WebGL, everything runs on a single main thread, so per-frame work built up there. Frequent network updates and redundant replicated state made it worse.

Implementation

I audited coroutine usage across the codebase, cut the update frequency and payload size, and removed replicated variables that were not needed.
