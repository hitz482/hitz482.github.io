Overview

HitzGAS Toolkit removes the setup boilerplate of the Gameplay Ability System. It ships as a precompiled plugin and works in Blueprint-only projects.

My role

I designed, built and documented all of it.

Main responsibilities

  • Three components: an attribute set initializer, an attribute listener and an Enhanced Input ability mapping.
  • A starter attribute set (health, mana, move speed, attack damage, shield) with damage reducing shield before health.
  • Sample abilities and effects, a live GAS debugger and a C++ AttributeSet generator.

Implementation

The debugger shows live attributes, granted abilities with activation counts, active effects and owned tags, and can export to JSON. The generator writes a compilable C++ AttributeSet with replication, OnRep handlers and clamping. Sample abilities cover instant line-trace damage, area-of-effect damage, a channeled ability with a tick effect and interruption by gameplay event, a root-motion dash with a cooldown, projectile spawning with an on-hit effect, and a tag-driven toggle. The abilities and effects are starting points meant to be read and adapted.
