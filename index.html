06 / About

About

I've been a gameplay programmer for more than five years, working in Unreal Engine 5 (C++ and Blueprints) and Unity (C#) on PC, mobile and VR titles. Most of that time has been at Fiftyfive Gaming Studio, in small teams of five or six with a modeller, an animator and a UI designer.

I'm most interested in gameplay architecture and multiplayer: deciding where authority lives, keeping systems modular, and building the tools that let other people extend them. When something goes wrong I measure first. The Ktrl Smash ping fix started with a graph of ping over a session, not a guess.

I'm based in Jaipur and work remotely across time zones.
